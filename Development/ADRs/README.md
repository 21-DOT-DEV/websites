# ADRs

Architecture decision records: one durable decision per file, `NNNN-slug.md`,
numbered in order and never reused. ADRs exist so that "why" survives after
the plan that produced the decision is finished.

## Conventions

- Frontmatter the checker validates: `adr`, `title`, `status` (`Proposed`,
  `Accepted`, `Superseded`), `date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), `supersedes` (ADR numbers
  this replaces), `superseded_by` (`NNNN` or `null`).
- Body sections: **Context** (the forces at play), **Decision** (what was
  decided), **Alternatives considered** (what was rejected and why),
  **Consequences** (what follows, good and bad).
- `Accepted` is final. A changed decision is a new ADR that supersedes the old
  one; the old file's `status` becomes `Superseded` and its `superseded_by`
  points at the replacement. The supersession chain is validated by the
  checker.
- Factual corrections (typos, stale paths, clarified wording) are edits, not
  new decisions — record them in the commit message. If the *decision*
  changed, that is a new ADR.
- A plan links the ADRs it relies on via its `adrs:` frontmatter field; the
  checker verifies both directions resolve.
